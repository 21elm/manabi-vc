Manabi Ventures

Manabi Ventures, based in Boston, is a venture capital firm with roots at the Harvard Innovation Labs (i-Lab). We provide venture capital financing to emerging and start-up companies. Our team have strong ties to the academic excellence of Harvard and MIT and have grown our network to include major U.S. cities like New York, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Miami, and Austin.

We specialize in helping startups from the very beginning, providing important support in sales, media, human resources, government relations, and fundraising. We use our expertise to help turn innovative ideas into successful businesses. At Manabi Ventures, we believe in giving practical and forward-thinking support to make a real impact.
